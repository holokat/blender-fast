What the system learned

Eight executable lessons describe arches, curved spans, branching crowns, window grids, spiral steps, revolved bowls, radial roofs, and hanging lanterns. A small local model selects relevant lessons; the agent combines and adapts them into a construction program.

Selecting a saved quality above changes the displayed image. It does not trigger a new render. The measurements exclude prompt interpretation and authoring. General ten-second text-to-scene generation has not been demonstrated.
